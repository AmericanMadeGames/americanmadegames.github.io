Highway Trivia

Do I need an account?

No. Download it, pick a category, and play. There are no accounts, no ads, and no data collected.

Will there be an Android version?

No Android version is planned. Google now requires every developer to verify their identity with Google before their apps can be distributed to Android devices, even free apps that collect nothing. That goes against what this studio stands for. If those rules change, we'll take another look.

I found a wrong answer in a question

Thank you! Email us the category and the question, and we'll fix it in the next update.

Torchlight

What is Torchlight?

A character sheet for the tabletop roleplaying game Dungeon World that runs in your browser. Open Torchlight.

Where are my characters saved?

In your own browser, on your own device. Nothing is sent to us, so we can't recover a character if your browser data is cleared. Characters also won't appear on a different device or browser.

Is this an official Dungeon World product?

No. Torchlight is an independent tool made by American Made Games and is not affiliated with Dungeon World's publishers.

Is there an iPhone app?

It's coming soon. Watch the news page for updates.

Gathered

iOS beta open
When can I get Gathered?

The iOS beta is open now through TestFlight, Apple's free app for testing new apps. Join the beta. Gathered will come to the App Store after testing.

Is it really free?

Yes, free now and forever, with no ads. Gathered was made as a service to the church, the body of Christ, just as salvation is a free gift.

"For it is by grace you have been saved through faith, and this not from yourselves; it is the gift of God." Ephesians 2:8 (BSB)

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Gathered works offline and never connects to a server.

Who can see our prayer requests?

Only the leader, on their own phone. Private requests stay private. A list of shared requests leaves the phone only when the leader copies it and chooses to send it to the group.

What happens if I delete the app or get a new phone?

Everything in Gathered lives only on your phone and is never sent to us, so we have no copy and can't restore it for you.
