Highway Trivia

Why there's no Android version

Google now requires every developer to verify their identity with Google before their apps can be distributed to Android devices — even free apps that collect nothing.

That requirement goes against what this studio stands for, and we can't reconcile the two. So no Android version is planned. If those rules change in the future, we'll take another look.
